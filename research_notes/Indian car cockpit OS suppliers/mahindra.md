## 1. MAIA cockpit on INGLO-platform EVs (BE 6, XEV 9e, XEV 9S; plus the XUV 3XO EV question)

### Takeaway
On the INGLO "electric origin" EVs, MAIA (Mahindra Artificial Intelligence Architecture) is Mahindra's own software layer. It runs on a Qualcomm Snapdragon Cockpit Platform: the SA8295P on the BE 6 and XEV 9e, and per secondary sources the SA8155P on the base XEV 9S. The strongest evidence on the OS is that the infotainment side is **Android Automotive OS (AAOS)**. A supplier (Cinemo) describes the Mahindra eSUV infotainment as "AAOS-based", and Mahindra's own job postings ask for AAOS HMI work on "domain controllers" and for GAS/AAOS certification. No public source names a cockpit tier-1 integrator for INGLO, a hypervisor, or the OS of the cluster partition. Mahindra appears to have integrated the cockpit itself with Qualcomm silicon. Aptiv supplies the displays, Harman the audio hardware, Cinemo the BYOD/media middleware and Google Cloud the Gemini voice agent.

### Inferences
- The infotainment side of MAIA is very likely AAOS (AOSP-derived Android Automotive), possibly with GAS certification in progress or in place. Supporting evidence: Cinemo's "AAOS-based", the Mahindra AAOS HMI jobs for "domain controllers", and the GAS/AAOS/XTS certification role. Counter-evidence: no Google Play or "Google built-in" branding has been reported, the apps are a curated set, and Google's Gemini release is a Cloud deal, not an Android Automotive one. My best guess is AAOS without GAS on cars shipped to date. The certification job suggests GAS may come later. **[Inferred]**
- Cluster partition: on SA8295P designs the cluster typically runs in a separate safety-oriented VM (QNX, or Linux under the Qualcomm/QNX hypervisor). The Mahindra AdrenoX precedent is QNX Hypervisor (see section 2), which suggests Mahindra may again use a QNX Hypervisor with an AAOS guest for IVI and passenger displays. This is not confirmed, and the Kanzi/Unreal skills in the job post fit cluster or 3D HMI rendering. **[Inferred]**
- No cockpit tier-1 (Visteon, Harman, LG or Bosch) is publicly tied to the INGLO cockpit domain controller. Mahindra seems to have built it in-house on Qualcomm silicon, with suppliers per component. Aptiv's 12.3-inch screens may be dumb displays fed over SerDes from one HPC. Autocar Pro's "high-performance computer in the infotainment system" supports a single central cockpit computer. **[Inferred]**
- For a phone-to-all-displays project, the most relevant integration point on INGLO cars is **Cinemo CARS Connect (BYOD)**. It already lets phones share media across multiple screens. An open project would probably need either a Mahindra partnership or AAOS app-level access. **[Inferred]**

### Gaps
- No source confirms the OS by name from Mahindra or Qualcomm. I also found no "About" or Settings screenshot showing an Android version, no developer-options or APK sideloading report, and no XDA or GitHub material for the BE 6, XEV 9e or XEV 9S.
- I found no hypervisor vendor, cluster OS or cluster rendering engine for the cluster partition.
- No app-store vendor is named (Harman Ignite, Faurecia Aptoide or other). The "60+ apps" (Qualcomm) are unattributed.
- No voice vendor before Gemini (for example Cerence) was found.
- **XUV 3XO EV / XUV400**: these are not INGLO cars (they are derived from ICE models). I found no source tying them to MAIA or naming their cockpit supplier.
- I found no teardown of the INGLO cockpit HPC.

## 2. AdrenoX / ADRENOX+ (XUV700 → XUV 7XO, Scorpio-N, Thar Roxx, XUV 3XO)

### Takeaway
AdrenoX was launched on the XUV700 in 2021. It is a **Visteon SmartCore cockpit domain controller** on a 3rd-gen **Qualcomm Snapdragon cockpit** SoC (SA8155P class). Its base layer is the **BlackBerry QNX Neutrino RTOS and QNX Hypervisor**. Owner reports suggest an **Android 9** guest for infotainment. **Bosch** built the AdrenoX Connect telematics and connected-car layer, **MapmyIndia (Mappls)** the navigation and **Amazon** Alexa Built-in. Sony audio was on the XUV700. The 2026 XUV 7XO facelift moves to **Visteon SmartCore Pro** ("ADRENOX+"): three 12.3-inch screens on SA8155P, Alexa with ChatGPT, 49 apps from Fun&Work, Harman Kardon audio and BYOD.

## 4. Mahindra software organisation, partnerships and openness

### Takeaway
Mahindra does cockpit HMI and AAOS work in-house, with HMI hiring in Coimbatore and Chennai (Mahindra Research Valley). It is led on the product-software side by Binoy Paul (Head, Product Development, Software Solutions and Technology). Its key cockpit partners are Qualcomm (silicon), Visteon (ICE CDCs), BlackBerry QNX (ICE OS base), Bosch (AdrenoX Connect), Google Cloud (Gemini), Amazon (Alexa), Cinemo (BYOD/media), Sibros (OTA) and Vector (middleware). Mahindra's openness is about BYOD and passenger-device control (Cinemo, "Theatre mode"), not a third-party app SDK.
